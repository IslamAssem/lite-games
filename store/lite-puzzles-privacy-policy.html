Privacy Policy

Lite Puzzles - Mini Games · Effective date: November 2, 2026

In short: Lite Puzzles - Mini Games does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The app works entirely offline on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Lite Puzzles - Mini Games (package com.islamassem.litepuzzles, the "App") handles information. The App is developed and published by Islam Assem ("we", "us", "the developer").

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any information. Specifically, the App:

  • does not require you to create an account or sign in;
  • does not request access to the internet, your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files;
  • does not contain advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs;
  • does not use device identifiers or advertising IDs;
  • does not offer in-app purchases.

2. Data stored on your device

Any game state (for example, the puzzle you are currently playing) stays on your device and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. If Android's built-in backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may include the App's local data in your personal device backup, which is governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours. Uninstalling the App removes its local data.

3. Sharing of information

Because we do not collect any information, we have nothing to share, sell or disclose to third parties.

4. Third-party services

The App is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information in connection with downloading and installing apps, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive personal information from Google about you.

5. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

6. Security

As no personal data leaves your device, there is no data held by us that could be exposed.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide or delete; you can remove all App data at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes how information is handled (for example, by adding online features), we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.

9. Contact
